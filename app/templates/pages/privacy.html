1. Who we are

This website is operated by {{ clinic.name }}, {{ clinic.locality }}, {{ clinic.area }}, {{ clinic.city }}, {{ clinic.state }}, India. You can contact us on {{ clinic.phone_display }}.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us through this website:

  • Appointment requests: your name, mobile number, optional email address, preferred date, session and time, and an optional short message.
  • Contact messages: your name, mobile number and/or email address, and your message.

Please do not send detailed medical history, reports or images through the website. These can be discussed and shared during your consultation.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Visitors to the public website are not given cookies. Clinic staff who sign in to the protected admin area receive a security cookie that is needed to keep them signed in.

To protect the website from abuse, we keep a short-lived, one-way-encrypted (hashed) form of the network address used to submit a form. Raw IP addresses are not stored with your request.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you about your appointment request or reply to your message.
  • To keep a record of requests so the clinic team can manage appointments.
  • To protect the website against spam and misuse.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

4. Your consent

When you submit a form, you agree that we may use the details you provide for the purposes above. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the clinic; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

5. Who can see your information

Only authorised clinic staff can see submitted requests, using individual password-protected accounts. Our hosting and email providers may process data on our behalf to run the website.
